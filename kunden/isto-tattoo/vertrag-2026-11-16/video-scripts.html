Isto Tattoo – video scripts (templates)

One short video for the ads, about 20 seconds. You film it once – only the offer sentence is filmed twice:

Version A: €300 off · Version B: 25 % off (both: first full-day session of a new large-scale project).

Speak English, naturally, in your own words – we add German subtitles. It does not have to be perfect.

How to film

  • Phone vertical (9:16), 1080p or 4K, camera at eye level, phone on a stand or held by someone
  • Good light (window or studio light from the front), quiet room, no music in the background
  • Look into the camera, not at the screen
  • Film each sentence 2–3 times, short pause before and after
  • Please also film the extra clips (list at the bottom) – we cut them in between
